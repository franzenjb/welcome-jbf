Welcome — Mapper & Red Cruiser

You need three systems. That is the whole first week. Profile. Docs. Maps.

01 — Volunteer Home

Volunteer Connection

This is your volunteer home. You keep your profile here. You say when you can go. You say how you want to help.

Log in. Click DCS Responder — that is the exact link name. Then set Availability (when you can go) and Intent (how you want to help).

  • Sign In volunteerconnection.redcross.org
  • DCS Responder Open after you log in
  • How-To Video intent.jbf.com/how-to
  1. Log In Use the Sign In link. This is the door.
  2. Click DCS Responder The link name is exact. Do not hunt for a close match.
  3. Set Availability and Intent Availability is when you can go. Intent is how you want to help.

02 — Intranet

One Source

One Source is the intranet. Job tools, documents, and policy live here. It is not Volunteer Connection. It is not the map.

Search is noisy. Do not wander. Bookmark the pages your supervisor points you to.

  • One Source Home onesource.redcross.org
  • Example Page onesource.redcross.org/page/8172

03 — ArcGIS Online

Maps / GIS

Mapping work lives in ArcGIS Online. The organization name is arc-nhq-gis. Sign in there. Train there. Work there.

Do not invent layers. Do not share Red Cross map data outside a signed-in ArcGIS Online session.
